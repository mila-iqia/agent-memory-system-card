# Agent Memory System Card — Feedback

Thanks for looking at the [Agent Memory System Card (AMS Card)](https://github.com/mila-iqia/agent-memory-system-card).

**Help us improve the template so it supports clear, transparent communication among memory-system developers, users, evaluators, and governance professionals!**

The AMS Card is about sovereign, measurable, portable memory. This survey applies the same discipline.

- **What we hold:** Only what you enter here. Country or territory, age range, and email address offer a “Prefer not to say” or blank option.
- **Where:** Mila's Google Workspace. Google's privacy practices apply to that layer.
- **Use:** Only to improve the AMS Card. Responses are not shared beyond this project team and are not used to train any model. Results may be anonymized and aggregated in research papers.
- **Retention:** 12 months after we publish the next version of the AMS Card. Responses are then anonymized or deleted.

The [live Google Form](https://forms.gle/TC8JLYi8YTZrmiDF9) is public and does not automatically collect email addresses.
